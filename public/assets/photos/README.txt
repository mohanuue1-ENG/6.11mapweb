Real photos for the public website. Drop a JPG here with exactly one of these names and the
website uses it instead of the illustration (no code change needed). Delete it to go back.

  hero.jpg   1600 x 1200 px (4:3). A truck on a road, daylight, truck clearly the subject.
  fleet.jpg  2000 x 800 px (5:2, wide). Your trucks lined up, or a driver beside a truck.

Tips: use your own trucks and drivers when you can (real beats stock), get permission from anyone
recognisable, keep each file under 400 KB (compress with squoosh.app), and avoid plates and company
names you do not want public.
